# mikeendrias-coder

**Attendee:** Michael Endrias (Intern), [@mikeendrias-coder](https://github.com/mikeendrias-coder)

**Project:** *Face Value*, an interactive guide to state facial recognition laws. It fits on one screen, styled after cdt.org, with pixel sprites as the accent.

- **Your state:** a scorecard of 7 common protections, with the law text behind each one
- **Follow a case:** pick who is searching, the photo, the database and the crime, then walk the search step by step past "gates" that show where state law steps in. The result step has "what if" options: pass the state's bills, or copy another state's law.
- **Compare:** two states, same case
- **Read the laws:** search and filter every law, bill and exact quote, and open the official text

## Files
- `index.html`: page structure
- `styles.css`: CDT colors and fonts (Epilogue, Geist), plus Silkscreen for pixel touches
- `app.js`: the app and the legal logic
- `data.js`: data from the State Facial Recognition Laws and Bills research (current through Oct. 26, 2026)
- `icons/`: pixel sprites in CDT colors, including the gate sprites
- `archive/`: the original hackathon version

Prototype, not legal analysis.
